Solar Park Suitability Analysis — Wetteraukreis & Vogelsbergkreis, Hessen
Multi-criteria GIS analysis identifying suitable locations for ground-mounted photovoltaic (GMPV) solar parks across Wetteraukreis and Vogelsbergkreis in Hesse, Germany — two deliberately contrasting districts (flat vs. hilly) used to stress-test the methodology.

197 scored sites were identified from 3,291 initial candidates, each ranked using an AHP-weighted multi-criteria score. Click any polygon for site details.
