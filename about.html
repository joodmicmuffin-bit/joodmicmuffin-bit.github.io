About Me

// background

I'm a Master's student in Sustainable Urban Development at The Technical University of Darmstadt, Germany. My academic journey began with a fully funded scholarship to study Policy Science at Ritsumeikan University in Japan, a country I had long been drawn to. Over five years, I learned the language, immersed myself in the culture, and built a foundation in urban planning, housing policy, community development, and governance.

I moved to Germany on another full scholarship to pursue my Master's, where those questions took a more technical turn. The MSc in Sustainable Urban Development bridges urban planning, civil engineering, and environmental science across regional and neighbourhood scales. Through coursework in sustainable building technologies, green building design, and spatial planning, I encountered GIS, and what started as a course requirement quickly became a genuine passion, and it's where my focus has stayed since.

Outside of academia, I enjoy painting and sketching at the park, and have been a video game fan since long before I started thinking seriously about cities :)
