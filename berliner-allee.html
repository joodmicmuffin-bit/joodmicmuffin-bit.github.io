// overview

This project assesses the energy performance of the Berliner Allee student residence in Darmstadt, a multi-storey reinforced concrete and masonry building, as part of TU Darmstadt's Green Building Design II module.

U-values for the wall, floor, and door were calculated from self-measured data and compared against GEG limits. The existing assemblies exceed permissible values, pointing to insufficient insulation across the building envelope.

Using PVGIS radiation data for Darmstadt, solar gains through the south-facing façade were modeled at the summer and winter solstice, confirming deeper winter sun penetration versus reduced summer midday gains.

A PVGIS rooftop simulation estimates ~262,232 kWh annual production, covering approximately 66% of the building's electricity demand.

The envelope underperforms current standards, but the building's orientation and roof show strong potential for solar retrofit.
